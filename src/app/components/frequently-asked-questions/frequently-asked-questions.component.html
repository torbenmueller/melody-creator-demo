Commercial License – FAQ

Everything you need to know about rights and usage

Who owns the melodies generated by the app?

You do. Any melody created while you have an active paid plan belongs to you and can be used in your projects.

Can I use the melodies commercially?

Yes. With a paid plan, you can use the melodies in videos, games, apps, ads, music releases, client work, and other commercial projects.

Will I keep my rights once my plan expires?

Yes. You keep full commercial rights to all melodies generated during your paid plan.

Can I sell the melodies as stock music or sample packs?

No. You may not resell the melodies as standalone products (e.g., sound packs, preset packs, stock libraries).

Can I modify or remix the melodies?

Yes. You can change them however you like and use your modified versions commercially.

Are free-tier melodies allowed for commercial use?

No. Melodies created on the free plan are for personal use only.

Do I need to credit the app?

No. Credit is appreciated but not required.
